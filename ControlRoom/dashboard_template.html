Layer 1 · Failure-mode taxonomy

Failures caught in time, by failure mode

Share of the held-out launches with that failure where an alert came in time: before ignition for countdown failures, before booster ignition for engine-start failures, at any point for flight failures. The fixed-limit set has no limit that responds to POGO or to a TVC limit cycle, so it scores zero there by construction.

Streaming replays of the 500 held-out launches, plus the classifier snapshot at the end of the window (H0 + 150 s). Median delay counts from the moment the simulator marks a failure as visible; a negative value means the agent saw it earlier than that conservative mark.

Knowledge universe

Every star is one action state: the failure-mode probabilities (one per failure mode) the agent held for one launch at one 0.5 s check. States the agent reads alike sit close together. A launch is a chain of states: point at a star to see its launch, click to keep it.
